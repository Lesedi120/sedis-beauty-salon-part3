OUR STORY

Beauty with a personal touch

At Sedi's Beauty Salon, we believe beauty services should help every client feel confident, cared for and comfortable in their own style.

Our approach

From hair installations and protective styles to makeup and nail care, our aim is to make your beauty appointment feel considered from consultation to finishing touch.

We encourage clients to share their preferred style, budget and any relevant hair or skin sensitivities before an appointment so that the service can be discussed clearly.

Our priorities

  • Friendly, respectful service
  • Clear service descriptions and starting prices
  • Careful attention to personal style
  • Clear communication before appointments
